## How to read this

- WER and CER use the normalizer in `docs/metrics.md`, applied to reference and hypothesis.
- The 95% CI comes from resampling utterances 1000 times. With few utterances it is wide. Differences smaller than the interval are not evidence of a real difference.
- Latencies are wall-clock seconds measured against the audio clock. They depend on the machine, the number of worker processes and, for hosted providers, the network.
